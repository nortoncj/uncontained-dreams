Zoning and permits

Texas rules depend on where your land sits.

  • Inside city limitsThe city's zoning and building codes apply. Check with your city's planning office before you buy.
  • Outside city limitsTexas counties generally can't zone, so rural land has fewer restrictions. Counties still regulate septic systems, floodplain building, and how land is divided, so you'll likely need a septic permit and a floodplain check.
  • HOAs and deed restrictionsCommon in master-planned communities around Houston. These can ban container homes even where the city allows them, so read yours first.

Not sure which applies to your land? Send us the address and we'll help you figure out who to call.
