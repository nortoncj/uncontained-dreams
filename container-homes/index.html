Is a container home right for you?

The honest version.

Container homes are a great fit for a lot of people and a bad fit for some. Here's both sides, so you can decide before you spend a dollar.

Why people choose them

  • Built in weeks in a shop, not a year on your lot
  • Steel structure that stands up to wind and weather
  • Arrives finished, so there's less work on your site
  • A smaller home that costs less to run
  • Can be moved later if your plans change

What to know going in

  • Rooms are narrower than a typical house, about 7.5 ft wide inside a single container
  • Steel needs good insulation, or it gets hot in summer and sweats in winter
  • Zoning rules vary, and a few towns don't allow them at all
  • Traditional mortgages are harder to get, so most buyers use other financing
  • Resale value depends a lot on your local market
