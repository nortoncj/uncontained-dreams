Zoning and permits

California wants you to build a backyard home.

State law limits how much cities can block an accessory dwelling unit (ADU). As of the state's July 2025 summary:

  • Most single-family lots can add one. A single-family lot can have one detached ADU, one ADU converted from existing space, and one junior ADU.
  • Size: Cities have to allow a detached ADU of at least 800 sq ft. A container home fits well under that. [CONFIRM: Rising Sun square footage]
  • Height: Detached ADUs can be at least 16 ft tall, more near transit.
  • Setbacks: Side and rear setbacks can't be more than 4 ft.
  • Speed: Once your application is complete, the city has 60 days to approve or deny it. If it doesn't act, the permit is deemed approved.
  • Fees: No impact fees on ADUs under 750 sq ft.
  • No owner-occupancy rule: You don't have to live on the property to add an ADU.

Local rules still apply on top of state law, so check with your city's planning department before you buy. This isn't legal advice.
