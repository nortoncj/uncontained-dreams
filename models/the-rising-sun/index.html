Overview

Everything a full-size home has. Nothing it doesn't need.

We designed The Rising Sun to prove a container home doesn't have to feel like a compromise. It has the full-size kitchen, the real shower, and the laundry you'd expect in a house. Off-the-shelf furniture didn't fit the space, so we designed and built our own: a futon that sleeps two, a TV stand that turns into a dining table, a bed with 40 cubic feet of storage. It all comes with the home.
